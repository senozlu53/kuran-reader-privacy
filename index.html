Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alemdar (“we”, “us”) handles information in the Kur'an-ı Kerim application for Samsung Tizen TV and our other supported platforms.

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Tizen TV version: the application is free, shows no advertising, and offers no in-app purchases or subscriptions on Tizen. Cloud sync is optional. You can read the Qur'an without creating an account or connecting a cloud provider.

1. Information we process

Depending on which features you use, the application may process:

  • Reading data: your last reading position, bookmarks, reading history, selected edition, theme, font size, and other app settings.
  • Device identifier: a random identifier generated by the application for sync and device management. It is not your hardware serial number, Samsung DUID, advertising ID, or TIFA.
  • Cloud account information: if you choose to connect Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Huawei Drive, or iCloud, we may receive an account label such as your email address or profile identifier, together with OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • Cloud sync data: the reading data described above is stored in your own cloud storage account when you enable sync. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.
  • Technical data: IP address and request metadata may be processed by cloud providers, OAuth providers, and hosting or relay services when the application communicates with them, for security and service operation.
  • Local diagnostics: the application may keep the last 20 sync events on your device. Tokens, email addresses, and search queries are removed before an event is stored. Diagnostics are not uploaded to us.

On iOS and Android, the free version may display non-personalized ads through Google AdMob. Tizen does not display ads and does not use TIFA. Payment information for optional mobile purchases is handled by the App Store or Google Play; we do not receive your card details.

2. Why we process information

  • To provide the reading experience and remember your preferences.
  • To synchronize your reading position, bookmarks, and settings across your devices when you ask us to.
  • To manage connected devices and protect against account mix-ups or unauthorized access.
  • To maintain security, diagnose failures, and provide support.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where required by law, we rely on your consent (for optional cloud connections), the performance of the service you requested, and our legitimate interest in keeping the application secure and functional.

3. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for third-party marketing. Information is disclosed only:

  • to the cloud provider you choose to connect, so sync can work;
  • to Apple or Google when you use their app store or payment services;
  • to Google AdMob for non-personalized ads on iOS and Android;
  • to hosting, relay, and security providers that operate the service;
  • when required by law, legal process, or to protect users and the service.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their own privacy policies apply to their processing.

4. Storage and retention

  • Local reading data remains on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the application.
  • Cloud sync data remains in your cloud account until you delete it there or disconnect sync.
  • OAuth tokens remain on the device until you disconnect the account, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Local diagnostics are limited to the last 20 events and remain on the device until cleared.

5. Security

We use transport encryption for network requests and platform secure storage for tokens where available. The web/TV build encrypts stored tokens with AES-GCM when the platform provides WebCrypto; otherwise the app avoids storing more than necessary. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

Cloud sync is optional. You can disconnect a provider in the app, clear local app data, or uninstall the app. You may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to processing, and withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. To make a request, contact us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The application is a general-audience Qur'an reading app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can address it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the application or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
